# Dyze's Pathogenics — Steam Workshop Description

## Your colonists can get sick. Now the sickness spreads.

Replace RimWorld's random illness events with a real disease that behaves like one — spreading through your colony before you even know it exists.

**Disease enters through visitors, traders, raiders, and refugees.** Infected outsiders bring illness to your doorstep. Once inside, it spreads through proximity: colonists sharing rooms accumulate exposure from infectious pawns. The catch? **Symptoms don't appear immediately.** There's an incubation period where infected colonists look perfectly healthy but can still make others sick.

That's the core tension: by the time you see disease, it's already throughout your colony.

---

## Features

- **Proximity-based transmission** — Disease spreads through shared air. Crowded rooms accelerate exposure.
- **Hidden progression** — Incubation period means disease can spread before visible symptoms appear.
- **Outsider importation** — Visitors, traders, raiders, refugees, prisoners, and quest pawns can introduce disease.
- **Mask protection** — Face-covering apparel (masks, gas helmets, full face gear) reduces or blocks transmission entirely.
- **Player notifications** — Alerted when colonists show symptoms, with info on likely transmission source.
- **Debug overlay** — See disease state for every pawn on the map.
- **Settings** — Toggle features, adjust import frequency and transmission rates.
